See how a support conversation gets triaged.

Paste or build a chat between a customer and an agent. The service works out what the customer needs and picks the next step the support policy allows. It also scores its own confidence and decides whether a person should take over. It only recommends: it cannot move money or change accounts.

  • Policy-bounded Actions come from a versioned allow-list
  • Calibrated Low confidence routes to a human
  • Injection-aware Chat text is data, never instructions
